FOR WHEN YOU NEED ME

Open When You're Sad

If this letter is opened, then I assume you are sad, and that's okay we are all allowed to be sad. Just know that it is also okay to not be okay, you don't always need to be strong and you are allowed to be sad sometimes. But also do remember that this moment of sadness will pass and that you won't always feel like this not for long anyway. Take some time to calm down, to collect your thoughts. Watch your comfort film or show, be cheeky, have some chocolate or something you enjoy but don't have often. Allow yourself to feel what you're feeling, don't push it down let yourself feel. And when you're ready to talk I am here for you and so are all of your friends. Don't ever feel like you have to be sad alone, I am more than happy to be with you when you do feel a bit blue. I hope this letter makes you feel somewhat better, and i am here for you always xoxo

Open When You Miss Me

Before I say anything I miss you too, more than words and actions can say. You are always always in my thoughts and most definitely in my heart all of the time, when we message and when we do not. Think about all the nice times we've had, all the films we've watched and all the times I've made you laugh. That's what I do when I miss you and it always brings a smile back on my face and I hope that it can do the same for you too. Those moments only make me look forward to creating more in the future, until then hold onto the memories we already do have knowing that they are a reminder of the love we share and the bond that keeps us connected. Dont miss me too much now, i am always here in spirit and if im not dont worry i am definitely thinking about you or reminiscing old messages in the bathroom crying xoxo

Also know that I am wishing I could do something to help you sleep and I do apologise for not being awake with you. Think of our happiest memories and funniest times hopefully those thoughts will comfort you. Perhaps imagine that you were in one of the forests we were gonna die in, think about us in there, what are we doing? let the thought take you away. Ill be here for when you wake up, ready to make new memories and make you laugh as per usual. Sleep well sugar xoxo

Open When You're Overthinking

i hope you dont have to open this letter but if you do then i hope i can help. If you haven't already message me, talk to me whats on your mind i will do everything in my power to help you. If talking doesn't work i want you to think about good memories no matter who they are about just something that will distract you from happening. And if that doesn't work try breathing in and out but also finding 5 things you can see, 4 things you can touch, 3 things you can hear, 2 things you can smell and lastly 1 thing you can taste. Here's a list of some things that might help you Cold water, or ice: hold something cold or splash your face with cold water Count backwards: from 100 or 50 or whatever number you wanna start from focus on a texture: touch something nearby and describe it, to yourself or to me or to whoever Object hunt: find 3 blue things or 4 round objects near you or in the room you are in I hope you are okay and that this helps sugar xoxo

Open When You're Having a Bad Day

It is tragic you had to open this and i feel sad to even write this i never want you to experience a bad day in your life but i suppose we all do. But why are you having a bad day? tell me whats going on. so that it wont happen again or just in case i need to fight anyone for you..But if you dont want to talk about it then thats okay too, just know that i am here for you if you do need and i will always be glad to listen to what you need to say. I am sorry if someone's upset you or if you're just having a bad day. Believe in yourself and don't let anyone say anything bad to make you not believe. Stay strong, take deep breaths and relax. It's just a bad day, not a bad life. A bad day is 24 hours and good thing we have tomorrow and tomorrow is a new day xoxo
